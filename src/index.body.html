Group rules

Separate businesses, with walls where they matter.

Own books, own targets

Every business has its own accounts and growth targets, reviewed every quarter.

No positions in print

Research never reveals live positions or names stocks to buy or sell, so readers can't be traded against.

No offers before proof

Signals and the Fund are not offered to anyone until the record is verified and lawyers sign off.

Same models for everyone

When Signals and the Fund open, licensees and investors get the same strategies the founders trade. No better version kept back.

FAQ

Questions about Accel Financial.

Questions about Trading or Research are answered on their own pages.

Why four separate businesses?

Each has different customers, different rules and a different way of making money. Keeping them separate means each can grow at its own pace, and a problem in one can't sink the others.

Does Accel manage anyone's money?

No. Accel Trading trades only the founders' own capital. Accel Fund will only be considered after an independent firm verifies the record.

Can I sign up for Signals or the Fund now?

No. Signals is still being built and the Fund doesn't exist; nothing on this site is an offer. Follow the monthly letter to watch the record build.

How is Accel Financial funded?

By the founders. Research sponsorships earn revenue early, which helps pay for the 24-month trading record.
