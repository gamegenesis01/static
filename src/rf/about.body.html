Editorial standards

The rules we don't bend.

No tips

We report the news. We never tell you what to buy or sell, and never promote a stock.

Our words, their link

We summarize other outlets in our own words and link to them. We never copy their articles.

No trading on our news

Accel's trading never acts on a story before readers receive it, and we never mention Accel's live positions.

Sponsors kept apart

Sponsor messages are labelled and never appear inside a news story.
