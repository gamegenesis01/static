Our rules

What licensees will be able to count on.

Same models as ours

The signals licensees get are the ones Accel trades with its own money. No better version held back.

Capacity is capped

We sell only as much capacity as the strategies can carry without their own trades eroding returns.

Changes announced first

Any model change is announced at least 5 trading days before it trades, and starts a new record.

No offers before proof

Nothing is offered or priced to anyone until the record is verified and our lawyers approve.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Allocators with research questions can reach us through the form below.

Can my firm license the signals now?

No. Accel Signals is not available and nothing on this page is an offer. Licensing will only be considered after an independent firm verifies the record and our adviser setup is complete.

Who will it be for?

Institutions such as hedge funds, asset managers and family offices that want to add a systematic, verified return stream to their own portfolios.

Why fingerprint signals before the open?

A signal published after the market moves proves nothing. A fingerprint published before the open proves the signal existed in exactly that form, so the record can't be cherry-picked later.

Will you publish the signals themselves?

Not in real time. The public sees fingerprints daily; the stored files go to the independent verifier, and later to licensees under contract.

Hear when it's verified.

One email when the independent check is published, plus the monthly letter. This is a notification list, not an offer.
