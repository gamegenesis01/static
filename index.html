    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Still curious? Our team replies in under two hours on market days.

    Where is my money actually held?

    In a brokerage account in your name at our independent custody and clearing partner. Accel directs the investments but never takes possession of your money.

    What is tax-loss harvesting?

    When an investment drops below what you paid, we sell it to record a loss and buy a similar one so you stay invested. That loss can offset gains and up to $3,000 of income a year on your taxes.

    Is there a minimum to start?

    You can start with $1 in Self-directed or Portfolios. Accel Direct starts at $100,000, Wealth at $500,000, and Accel Private funds from $10,000 for accredited investors.

    Can I move my existing account?

    Yes. Transfer a brokerage account, IRA or 401(k) rollover in a few clicks. We usually move your holdings as they are, so you don't sell and trigger taxes, and we cover transfer fees up to $150.

    Can I lose money?

    Yes. All investing involves risk, including the loss of what you put in. Diversification and a long time horizon reduce the risk, but they don't remove it.
