    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Still curious? Our team replies in under two hours on market days.

    Do your bots always beat the market?

    No strategy wins every year, and ours won't either. Each one is built to beat its benchmark over a full market cycle, inside strict loss limits. There will be months and years when it trails.

    Where is my money held?

    Managed accounts are held in your name at an independent custodian. With AI Connect, your money stays at your own broker. In both cases we can place trades but can't withdraw your money.

    What happens if the AI makes a mistake?

    Every order passes pre-trade risk checks first. Your own loss limit stops the bots automatically, and our risk desk can pause any strategy instantly.

    Can I withdraw my money?

    Yes, any business day. Positions are sold and the cash usually reaches your bank within two to three business days.

    Which brokers work with AI Connect?

    AI Connect works with major U.S. brokers that offer trading API access. We'll confirm your broker during sign-up.
