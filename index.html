    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Still curious? Our team replies in under two hours on business days.

    Is Accel a bank?

    No. Accel Financial Services is a financial technology company. Banking services are provided by our regulated partner banks, which hold your deposits.

    Who can open an account?

    Companies incorporated in the U.S., U.K. or E.U., from pre-seed startups to public companies. Sole proprietors are supported on the Launch plan.

    How does Accel Treasury earn yield?

    Balances above a threshold you set are swept into a portfolio of short-term U.S. Treasury bills. You can move money back to your operating account any business day, usually arriving the same day.

    Can I keep my current bank?

    Yes. Many customers start by connecting their existing bank to Accel Pilot for reconciliation, then move operating cash over when they are ready.

    What does Global Pay cost?

    The mid-market exchange rate plus a flat 0.25%, with no hidden spread. Domestic transfers in the U.S., U.K. and Eurozone are free.
