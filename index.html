    How we prove it

    Three rules that make cherry-picking impossible.

    Anyone can show a great chart. The hard part is proving it wasn't picked after the fact. Here is how we make our record hard to fake.

    Rule 1

    100%own capital

    Our money, at risk first

    Every trade in the record is made with the founders' own money at a regular brokerage. Paper trading and back-tests don't count toward the record.

    Rule 2

    2026-11-12 16:05 ET 9f3a71c0…e84bc21e 214 trades · published

    Every day locked in

    At each close we publish a fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash) of that day's full trade log. Change a single trade later and the fingerprint no longer matches.

    Rule 3

    3rdparty checks

    Someone else does the maths

    An independent firm matches our logs to broker statements and calculates returns. We publish their report, good or bad.

    Risk control

    Machines make the trades. Humans hold the keys.

    An edge only matters if you survive long enough to use it. Every strategy runs inside limits we wrote down before day one, and published.

    Pre-trade limits

    Position size, sector exposure and leverage are checked before every order. If a check fails, the order isn't sent.

    If a strategy hits its loss limit, it goes to cash automatically and stays there until we've written up what happened.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Something we haven't covered? Ask, and we'll answer it in the next letter.

    Can I invest with Accel now?

    No. We are not accepting outside money and nothing on this site is an offer to sell securities. We'll only consider a fund after an independent firm has verified our live record.

    Why should I believe your results?

    You shouldn't have to take our word for it. Each day's trade log is fingerprinted and published before results are known, our own money is at risk, and an independent firm checks the numbers against broker statements.

    What if the bots lose money?

    Then you'll read about it in that month's letter. Every strategy has a published loss limit. If a strategy fails over the full period, we'll say so plainly and won't launch it.

    Why not show back-tests instead?

    Back-tests are built with hindsight and almost always look better than live trading. We use them to research ideas, never as proof.

    I run a fund. Can we license the signals?

    We'll start those conversations once 12 months of the record are verified. Leave your details below and mention your firm.
