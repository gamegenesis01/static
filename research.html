How we work

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Reply to any issue.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Daily Brief is free and paid for by clearly labelled sponsors.

How do you publish so early?

Our research systems follow filings, data releases and company news as they land overnight, and our editor checks every issue before it's sent.

Is this investment advice?

No. Accel Research publishes general market news for everyone. It never recommends a security and isn't advice for your situation.

Does Accel trade on the news it publishes?

Accel's bots never trade on a story before readers receive it, and the brief never mentions Accel's live positions.

Can I unsubscribe?

Anytime, with one click at the bottom of every email.
